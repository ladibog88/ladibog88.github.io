Travel Bees

Travel Logging Bot + Live Scrapbook

A Telegram bot initially built for a 6-week journey through South America, but now rolled out for all types of trips. Send a photo, a Google Maps link, or a daily reflection — the bot captures it, stores it in Google Sheets, and publishes everything to a live scrapbook with an interactive map.

Python Telegram Bot API Google Sheets Cloudinary Leaflet.js Railway Cloudflare Pages

The Story

Min and I had a 6-week trip to Peru and Ecuador lined up. We both wanted to document it properly. Not just Instagram posts after the fact, but a real-time record and timeline: where we were, what we ate, what we thought, what surprised us.

I built Travel Bees before we left. The idea was simple: a Telegram bot that required zero friction to use. Take a photo — send it. Drop a Google Maps link from the restaurant you just loved — send it. Type a thought before you sleep — send it. Everything gets captured, timestamped, and geolocated automatically.

The hard deadline of May 31st departure focused every design decision perfectly.
